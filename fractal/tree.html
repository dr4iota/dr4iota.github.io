Tree

A trunk, fixed from its base up to a branch point, leaves that point as several branches, and each branch repeats the trunk at its own scale and direction. A branch repeats the picture from its tip. With one branch the trunk just continues, and with three, fanned out, it is the outline of a leaf or a fern frond.

Where the branches leave the trunk, they spread along its top: the outer ones reach its corners and the ones between are spaced evenly, each turned a little so its tip stays where it is dragged. A branch too wide for the top wraps over the trunk's corner, and one that would cut back into the trunk is left out, with everything above it. Every branch repeats this, so the whole tree stays an exact copy of itself.

Each branch end is a draggable point. Its distance from the branch point gives the branch's length, and its direction gives its angle. The depth slider sets how many levels are drawn. The deepest allowed falls as the branch count rises, since each level has more copies.
