Games

Telechore carries eighteen of Termoasis's console games — 2048, Sudoku, Mines, Snake, Life and more — drawn in the terminal's characters. They run in the app itself, with no server and no connection, on every platform.

Games is the last section of the start page and the pane. Its one row, Termoasis console games, opens the welcome menu.

The welcome menu and the picker

The welcome menu lists every game by kind: puzzles, arcade games and simulations. Move to one and press Play (or Enter) to start it.

The name at the top of the page is also the picker: tap it to switch to any game, mid-game too, or back to Welcome. Up, left of the name (or first in ⋯), quits the game and brings the welcome menu back, on the game you left.

Playing

On a touch screen each game draws its own keys under the board, or beside it when the screen is held sideways: the arrows on the right, the game's own actions (Undo, Restart, Place, Drop…) on the left, and a number pad for the games that take numbers. With a keyboard the keys are hidden and the game's own keys work; Keys in the header shows or hides them either way.

Some games are steered by a finger on the board: drag in Paddles to move the paddle.

Levels and modes

A game's levels, difficulty or mode — Warehouse's levels, Mines' difficulty, Chaos's mode — is a stepper: ‹ and › step through them, and a tap on its value lists them all. It stands in the header where there is room, else in a band under it, or beside the keys when the screen is turned; ⋯ lists every setting a game has, each opening its list. The game's own keys still work, and on a command line set level 12 does the same.

Help shows the game's own help: its keys, and the commands it takes. Command opens a line to type one into: family julia in Fractals, pattern glider in Life. Each game's help says which commands it has.

A game that needs more room than the screen has is drawn smaller, down to a font that still reads, or says how much it needs: Mines on Expert, on a phone held upright, is the one you are likely to meet.

Where the header has room, Keep, Keys, Help and Command stand in it. On a narrow screen they are behind ⋯, or beside a game's level under the header; turned, at the top of the keys where there is room.

Keeping a game

Keep, in the header (the anchor), keeps a game running while you are elsewhere, as it keeps a terminal: leave the page, go Up, or pick another game, and the kept one waits where you left it; come back to it from the strip of Kept games on the welcome menu, or from a terminal's list. A game you did not keep closes when you leave it, and one that has ended is replaced by the next.

Kept games do not count against the terminals a store copy keeps, and quitting the app does not ask about them.

A kept game lives as long as the app does: closing the app, or Android closing it in the background, ends it, as it ends a kept terminal. What a game saves survives: every game's best score.

In a browser

The same games play on Telechore's website, under Play, in any current browser. There they keep their saves in the browser.
