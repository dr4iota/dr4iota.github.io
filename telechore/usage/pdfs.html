These pages are for Telechore 0.5.2 — what's new in it.

Reading PDFs and pictures

A PDF opens in Telechore's own viewer, the same on every device: the pages one under another at the width of the window, in the app's light or dark colours. In dark mode the text and lines take the dark theme's colours and keep their hue; the pictures in the PDF keep theirs. The page you are on shows at the bottom right while you scroll. Words can be selected and copied. Scanned pages are drawn too, including black-and-white scans and faxes (JBIG2 and CCITT) and JPEG 2000 pictures.

Source, beside Preview in the header, shows the PDF's size, date and version, and the start of the file in hex.

Zoom and pan

The same as in the terminal:

  • Pinch to zoom, and drag with two fingers to move around a page larger than the window. One finger scrolls.
  • On a computer, Ctrl+wheel zooms (Cmd on a Mac, and a trackpad's pinch), Ctrl+drag moves the page, and the plain wheel scrolls. Ctrl+= and Ctrl+− zoom a step.

Reset view

Hold two fingers still on the page until the note at the top says Release to reset view, then lift them; or Ctrl+double-click, or Ctrl+0. Each reset goes on to the next of three views, keeping the page you are reading:

  1. Full width — the page across the window, as it opens.
  2. Margins cut — the paper around the text cut away on all four sides, so the words fill the window and the pages run on with little between them. Zooming and moving keep the cut.
  3. Whole page — the whole page in the window at once.

A view that would look the same as one before it is passed over: on a phone held upright the whole page is the full width, so a reset goes between Full width and Margins cut. After you zoom or move, the next reset goes back to Full width.

Links

A link to a website or an email address opens in your browser or mail app, as a link in a Markdown file does. A link to another page of the PDF goes there. Links to anything else — a file, a program — are not followed.

Pictures

A picture opens the same way: zoom and move it as a PDF, and Reset view goes on to the next of three views, each centred:

  1. Fit — the whole picture in the window, as it opens.
  2. Fill — the picture covering the window, the rest of it a drag away.
  3. Actual size — the picture at 100 %, one of its pixels to a pixel of the app's page, as a browser shows it at 100 %.

A view that would look the same as one before it is passed over.

Full screen

Full screen, beside Refresh in the header, shows the PDF or the picture alone, on the whole screen. A tap shows the way out at the top right, the file's name, size and date at the top left, and at the bottom right the page you are on, or for a picture its size in pixels and its view (4032 × 3024, Fill · 140 %); a tap while they show hides them all. On a phone, nothing is under the status bar or the cutout until you scroll or zoom. Zoom and Reset view work as before. To leave, tap the way out, press Esc or F11, or Back.

What it does not do yet

A PDF with a password says it cannot be opened.
