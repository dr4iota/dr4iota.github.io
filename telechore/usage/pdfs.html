These pages are for Telechore 0.6.0 — what's new in it.

Reading PDFs and pictures

A PDF opens in Telechore's own viewer, the same on every device: the pages one under another at the width of the window, in the app's light or dark colours. In dark mode the text and lines take the dark theme's colours and keep their hue. Figures and scans that are mostly white turn dark too, their colours kept; photographs keep theirs (see “Darken figures and scans” below). The page you are on shows at the bottom right while you scroll. Words can be selected and copied. Scanned pages are drawn too, including black-and-white scans and faxes (JBIG2 and CCITT) and JPEG 2000 pictures.

A PDF you have read before opens where you left it: the same view, page, place and zoom. A new one opens at Full width, at the top.

“Source”, beside “Preview” in the header, shows the PDF's size, date and version, and the start of the file in hex.

Zoom and pan

The same as in the terminal:

  • Pinch to zoom, and drag with two fingers to move around a page larger than the window. One finger scrolls only the way the pages run: up and down, or across in “Whole page”, so the page does not slide sideways while you read.
  • On a computer, Ctrl+wheel zooms (Cmd on a Mac, and a trackpad's pinch), Ctrl+drag moves the page, and the plain wheel scrolls. A drag with the mouse selects words. Ctrl+= and Ctrl+− zoom a step.

Reset view

Hold two fingers still on the page until the note at the top says Release to reset view, then lift them; or Ctrl+double-click, or Ctrl+0. Each reset moves to the next of three views, keeping the line you are reading:

  1. “Full width” — the page across the window, as it opens.
  2. “Margins cut” — each page with the paper around its text cut away, so the words fill the window. With “Single column view” on, this view is “Single column” instead (see below).
  3. “Whole page” — each page whole in the window, the pages side by side. Swipe with one finger, or use the wheel or the arrow keys, to turn a page; it stops on one page at a time. Zoomed in, one finger moves freely along the pages.

A view that would look the same as one before it is passed over. After you zoom or move, the next reset goes back to Full width. The three views are also in the “Options” menu.

Options

“Options”, in the header between “Full screen” and “Send”, opens the PDF's menu. The button is marked “PDF”.

  • “Single column view” — see below.
  • “Heads, feet and page numbers” — shown or hidden in the Margins cut and Single column views. Hidden, each page is still a box of its own.
  • “Darken figures and scans” — in dark mode only, for this file; greyed out in light mode. On, a picture that is mostly white, or drawn in a few flat colours, such as a chart or a scanned page, turns dark with its colours kept. Off, pictures keep their own colours.
  • “Mode” — the three views: “Full width”, “Margins cut” or “Single column”, and “Whole page”. Where the menu is narrow they read “Width”, “No margins” or “Column”, and “Page”.

Single column view

For papers and magazines set in two or three columns. Each column is shown at the full width of the window, one after another in reading order, so you read straight down without zooming or moving across. A title or a figure across the columns comes first, as on the page; running heads and page numbers go above and below. One page's parts stay in one box, with a dashed line between them.

It is chosen for each file in “Options”, and Telechore remembers the choice. It is greyed out for a file it cannot help: one with a single column throughout, or one whose layout cannot be read. Switching it on moves to the Single column view at once. Switching it off goes back to Margins cut.

Telechore finds the columns itself, page by page. It aims to leave a page whole rather than cut it wrongly, so an unusual page may stay as it is.

Single column view is part of Pro. A copy from an app store can use it on 28 PDFs for free. A file uses one of those 28 the first time you switch the view on, and switching it off does not give that one back. The menu says how many are left, and “Unlock Pro” on that line opens the offer. With Pro, or in a copy you installed from a downloaded file, there is no limit. See The free edition and Pro in Servers.

Links

A link to a website or an email address opens in your browser or mail app, as a link in a Markdown file does. A link to another page of the PDF goes there. Links to anything else — a file, a program — are not followed.

Pictures

A picture opens the same way: zoom and move it as a PDF, and “Reset view” goes on to the next of three views, each centred:

  1. “Fit” — the whole picture in the window, as it opens.
  2. “Fill” — the picture covering the window. The part that does not fit is a drag away.
  3. “Actual size” — the picture at 100 %, one of its pixels to a pixel of the app's page, as a browser shows it at 100 %.

A view that would look the same as one before it is passed over.

Full screen

“Full screen”, beside “Refresh” in the header, shows the PDF or the picture alone, on the whole screen. A tap shows a button to leave at the top right, the file's name, size and date at the top left, and at the bottom right the page you are on. For a picture that corner shows its size in pixels and its view (4032 × 3024, Fill · 140 %). A tap while those are showing hides them all. For a PDF the tap also shows, at the bottom left, the “Options” button and the three views, one tap each. On a phone the page stays clear of the status bar and the camera cutout until you scroll or zoom. Zoom and reset view work as before. To leave, tap the button, press Esc or F11, or Back.

What it does not do yet

Telechore cannot open a PDF that asks for a password, and it says so.
